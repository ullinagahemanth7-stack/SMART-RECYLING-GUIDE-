Three habits that make recycling work

Separate at home

Wet and dry waste mixed together can't be recycled. Two buckets in the kitchen is enough to start.

Rinse, drain, flatten

Food-soiled paper and plastic gets rejected. A quick rinse and squashing containers saves space.

Hand over, don't dump

Give dry waste to your waste collector, kabadiwala or society collection point instead of burning or dumping it.

Where to take it

Municipal collection

Door-to-door vans take wet and dry waste. Ask your ward office for the hazardous and sanitary pickup schedule.

Kabadiwala / scrap dealer

Pays for newspaper, cardboard, metal, glass bottles and PET bottles. Good for clean dry waste.

Authorised e-waste recycler

E-waste must go to an authorised recycler or a brand take-back programme under India's E-Waste (Management) Rules. Check your state pollution control board's list.

Composting

Home compost bins, community composters or society composting pits turn wet waste into manure.
